Testing for trends in online experiments


Chris Haulk, Lee Richardson, Jacopo Soriano
[stat.ME]

Time-varying treatment effects can be estimated with linear regression paired with jackknife confidence intervals. In experiments at a large online video platform, the test based on this estimator is not much less powerful than modern tests for monotonic trends. If you suspect trends in your online experiments, consider using this estimator.
