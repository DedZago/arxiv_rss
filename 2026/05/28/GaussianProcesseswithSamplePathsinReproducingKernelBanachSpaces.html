Gaussian Processes with Sample Paths in Reproducing Kernel Banach Spaces


Toni Karvonen, Rasmus Kleist H{\o}rlyck S{\o}rensen
[stat.ML]

We investigate the connection between Gaussian processes and Gaussian random elements in reproducing kernel Banach spaces. We show that the covariance operator of a weak second-order Radon probability measure on such a space is uniquely determined by a positive definite function. In the Gaussian case, we characterize those positive definite functions that arise from covariance operators in terms of $\gamma$-radonifying operators. Building on these results, we extend the classical Driscoll theorem to the Banach space setting.
