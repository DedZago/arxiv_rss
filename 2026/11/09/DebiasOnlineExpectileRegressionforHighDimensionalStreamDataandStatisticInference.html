Debias Online Expectile Regression for High-Dimensional Stream Data and Statistic Inference


Wei Cao, Shanshan Wang
[stat.ME]

Expectile regression is a powerful tool for capturing heterogeneous structures in data and has attracted increasing attention in large-scale data analysis due to its computational efficiency. However, statistical inference for high-dimensional online expectile regression remains underexplored. In this paper, we propose a debiased online expectile regression method for high-dimensional streaming data. The proposed method incorporates Lasso regularization to perform variable selection and alleviate estimation instability caused by high dimensionality. It further adopts an online updating strategy in which recursively updated cumulative statistics are used to approximate the global gradient, and a proximal gradient descent algorithm is employed to update model parameters. To enable valid inference when only the current batch and historical summary statistics are available, we also develop an online precision matrix estimation procedure. We establish the consistency and asymptotic normality of the proposed debiased estimator. Extensive numerical studies and real-data applications show that the proposed method achieves performance comparable to the oracle and offline estimators while maintaining low storage cost.
