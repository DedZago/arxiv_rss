Kernel-Based Learning of Analytic Operators and Parametric PDEs


Giovanni S. Alberti, Markus Holzleitner, Lorenzo Rosasco, Matteo Santacesaria
[stat.ML,stat.TH]

We study kernel-based learning of analytic operators from finite samples, with a focus on how the regularity of the target should determine the choice of the kernel. We establish embeddings of analytic operators into vector-valued reproducing kernel Hilbert spaces and derive finite-sample, high-probability guarantees that quantify the uncertainty due to random sampling: in the noiseless setting, suitable analytic kernels yield rates exponential in a fractional power of the sample size, while Sobolev-type kernels yield polynomial rates. We apply the theory to the solution operator of a parametric elliptic PDE, where the training data are themselves finite-element approximations, and obtain bounds that separate the sampling and discretization contributions to the error and prescribe how the mesh size should be coupled to the sample size. The results are illustrated numerically.
