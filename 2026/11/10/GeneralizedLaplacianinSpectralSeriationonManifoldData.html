Generalized Laplacian in Spectral Seriation on Manifold Data


Ruizi Wu, Wanjie Wang, Jinchi Lv
[stat.ME]

Noisy high-dimensional observations may have conditional means lying on a smooth closed curve, indexed by temporal labels or phases. We want to recover the latent phases for each observation and thus retrieve a circular ordering. Spectral seriation uses the graph Laplacian’s spectral embedding to recover the phases and ordering. We consider generalized graph Laplacians in spectral seriation, where a parameter $\alpha$ controls the degree normalization and affects the accuracy and stability of both tasks. We develop and compare three generalized Laplacian constructions: a shifted generalized Laplacian, a graph-difference Laplacian, and an extra-normalized generalized Laplacian. Among these, the extra-normalized variant is less sensitive to the choice of $\alpha$ and achieves the strongest overall recovery performance. For this variant, we establish a theoretical error bound relating phase estimation accuracy to eigenspace perturbation, accounting for the inherent rotation and reflection ambiguity. In synthetic experiments across various scenarios, our new graph Laplacian outperforms the classic spectral seriation method and other seriation methods. On the Pink Angel data, which records photos from different viewpoints, our method achieves zero ordering error and the lowest phase recovery error. On FUCCI cell-cycle data, our method performs the best when all 6 cells are mixed, demonstrating the robustness to heterogeneity.
