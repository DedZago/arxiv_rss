Numerical and analytical strategies to propagate correlated uncertainties through the discrete Fourier transform


Tuguldur Zolzaya, Andrew R. McCluskey
[stat.AP,stat.CO]

The Fourier transform underpins a range of comparisons between atomistic simulations and scattering measurements, yet uncertainty propagation through it is seldom discussed, and correlations between data points are usually ignored. Here, we give a pedagogical account of uncertainty propagation through the discrete Fourier transform, including the full covariance matrix. Two routes are described: a numerical scheme that samples from the multivariate normal distribution describing the data, and an analytical scheme that treats the transform as a change of basis. We discuss the scaling behaviour of these methods and demonstrate their utility on problems that link simulation to scattering: the Faber–Ziman partial structure factors of liquid water, and the dynamic structure factor obtained from a rotational autocorrelation function.
