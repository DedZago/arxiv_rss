Tail behaviour of infimum of Gaussian processes


K. Debicki, E. Hashorva, S. Novikov
[stat.ME]

We investigate the upper tail of $\inf_{t\in\mathcal T}X(t)$, where $X$ is a centred Gaussian process (GP) with continuous sample paths on a nonempty compact metric space $\mathcal T$. As suggested by the findings of [1], the exponential rate is governed by an optimal-measure problem, while the exact prefactor depends on the local geometry of the corresponding contact set. In the full-contact regime, we strengthen the known Gumbel max-domain of attraction property of the distribution of $\inf_{t\in\mathcal T}X(t)$ by establishing the von Mises condition. We illustrate our findings with examples including fractional Brownian motion and the fractional Brownian sheet.
