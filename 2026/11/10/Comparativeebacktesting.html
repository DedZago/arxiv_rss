Comparative e-backtesting


Zhanyi Jiao, Qiuqi Wang, Yimiao Zhao
[stat.ME,stat.AP]

Backtesting risk measures is a central task in financial regulation. While traditional backtests evaluate whether a forecasting model is statistically consistent with observed losses, regulatory practice often requires assessing the performance of an internal model relative to standard models. In this paper, we develop a parametric anytime-valid framework for comparative backtests of general risk measures using e-values and e-processes. We construct likelihood-based e-processes for comparative e-backtests with validity and power guarantees. As practical guidelines, we propose the e-three-zone approach based on weak dominance, and introduce the overall and resetting test schemes for different practical purposes. Simulation studies and empirical analyses are conducted to show practical manipulations in financial regulation.
