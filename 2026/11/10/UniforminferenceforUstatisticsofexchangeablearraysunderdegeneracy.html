Uniform inference for $U$-statistics of exchangeable arrays under degeneracy


Tâm LeMinh
[stat.ME,stat.TH]

Many $U$-statistics of row-column exchangeable arrays become degenerate at natural null hypotheses: their convergence rates change and their limiting laws may or may not remain Gaussian. We establish conditions under which inference based on the classical unbiased variance estimator is uniformly valid across these changes, without knowledge of the degeneracy order. It is known that $U$-statistics can be decomposed into orthogonal projections indexed by bipartite graphs. Under uniform fourth-moment assumptions, we establish that the standardized statistic is asymptotically Gaussian and the classical unbiased variance estimator is ratio-consistent, as long as a certain measure of concentration for projections indexed by disconnected graphs tends to zero. Under compactness of the normalized projections, both properties hold if and only if the proportion of variance contributed by disconnected graphs tends to zero. The necessity of this condition follows from a general limit theorem for these $U$-statistics, in which each graph contributes to a Wiener chaos of order equal to its number of connected components. We note that the row-column jackknife variance estimator, although nonnegative, does not generally give a studentized statistic with a standard normal limit in degenerate regimes. We illustrate our theory with an application to estimation and testing based on a distance from product form, exhibiting Gaussian and non-Gaussian limits at various rates depending on the model. We also propose a conservative inference procedure that does not require a Gaussian limit, valid under additional moment and projection conditions.
