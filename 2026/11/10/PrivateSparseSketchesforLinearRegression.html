Private Sparse Sketches for Linear Regression


Shrutimoy Das, Anirban Dasgupta
[stat.ML]

Sketching compresses a large dataset into a smaller summary, allowing regression on the sketch to approximate the full-data objective. A sketch released under differential privacy can be reused with any non-private regression solver, any number of times, without further privacy loss. We study private sparse sketches for $\ell_2$ and $\ell_1$ regression. We first prove lower bounds on the noise required by common sparse sketches for both problems when the sketching matrix is public. Since regression on a sketch can be solved without revealing the sketching matrix, we construct sketches whose randomness is kept secret. Hiding how individual records enter the sketch provides an additional source of privacy, allowing less noise for the same privacy guarantee. We prove differential privacy guarantees for both constructions, and experiments on real datasets show improved utility over public-sketch baselines.
