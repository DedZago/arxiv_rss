Nonasymptotic causal structure learning in generalized linear DAGs with continuous optimization


Chang Deng, Inbeom Lee, Bryon Aragam
[stat.ME,stat.ML,stat.TH]

We study the problem of learning directed acyclic graphs from observational data in nonlinear models and provide finite-sample, nonasymptotic statistical guarantees in these models. Although widely studied, statistical guarantees for gradient-based methods in nonlinear models are missing from the literature. Under a generalized linear model for each node, we analyze a global nonsmooth objective combining an exact acyclicity constraint with a quasi-minimax concave penalty, which can be optimized via standard gradient-based solvers. We establish finite-sample guarantees under this model, including the most widely used logistic and Poisson models. Experiments corroborate structure recovery accuracy and computational performance, including both standardized data and negative controls as baselines.
