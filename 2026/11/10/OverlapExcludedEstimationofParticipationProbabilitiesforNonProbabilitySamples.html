Overlap-Excluded Estimation of Participation Probabilities for Non-Probability Samples


Lyndon Ang, Robert Clark, Bronwyn Loong, Anders Holmberg
[stat.ME]

Correction of selection bias is crucial for making valid statistical inferences about a finite population using non-probability datasets. Various approaches have been proposed to correct this bias and obtain estimates of finite population quantities such as means and totals. The quasi-randomisation approaches, in particular, aim to estimate a `participation probability’ for units in the non-probability dataset. These probabilities are then applied in an inverse probability weighting scheme to produce population estimates. In this paper, we propose to estimate the participation probabilities for the non-probability sample by combining the non-probability sample with an available probability sample, while excluding the overlapping units in the probability sample. When the true overlap cannot be determined, we show how the Expectation-Maximization method can be used to simultaneously estimate the overlap and the participation probabilities. We compare the performance of our approach against several alternative methods theoretically and in an empirical context, and demonstrate the efficiency gains that are possible when the overlapping probability sample is excluded.
