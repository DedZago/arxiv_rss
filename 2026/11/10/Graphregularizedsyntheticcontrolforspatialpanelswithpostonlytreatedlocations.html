Graph-regularized synthetic control for spatial panels with post-only treated locations


Hajime Seya, Ryosei Moriwaki, Daisuke Murakami
[stat.ME]

Spatial policy panels can contain treated locations that are observed only after an intervention and therefore have no pre-treatment outcome history. Without a pre-treatment trajectory to match, choosing donor weights for such post-only locations requires an additional source of information. We propose Graph-SCM, which estimates donor weights jointly across treated locations and encourages geographically neighboring locations to use similar donor combinations. GeoEco-SCM keeps the same geographic graph but adjusts the strength of its existing edges using pre-treatment economic similarity. Both methods handle complete, partial, and absent histories within one convex weight-estimation problem. Monte Carlo experiments compare them with covariate-balanced synthetic controls, equally weighted donor averages, ordinary synthetic control where estimable, and nonseparable spatio-temporal imputation and direct prediction. No method dominates. Graph-based borrowing improves on covariate balancing in several settings, but a selective-coverage design favors covariate balancing. Economic adjustment helps when geography alone misses relevant donor relationships. The results support graph regularization as a way to extend synthetic-control coverage when connected locations share similar donor representations, with its usefulness depending on the structure of the available information.
