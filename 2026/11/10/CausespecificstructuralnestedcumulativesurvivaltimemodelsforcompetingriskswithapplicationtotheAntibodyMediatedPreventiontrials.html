Cause-specific structural nested cumulative survival time models for competing risks, with application to the Antibody Mediated Prevention trials


Yuxiao Li, Yuhao Deng, Bo Zhang
[stat.ME]

The Antibody Mediated Prevention (AMP) trials randomized participants to VRC01, a broadly neutralizing antibody, or placebo; the primary analysis estimated prevention efficacy against overall HIV-1 acquisition and against acquisition of VRC01-sensitive strains. All participants were offered daily oral pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP), and PrEP uptake in a trial may differ substantially from uptake in other settings. It is therefore of interest to estimate the effect of oral PrEP on acquisition of VRC01-sensitive and VRC01-insensitive strains, as well as the counterfactual cumulative incidence of such acquisition under no or limited PrEP uptake. To address these questions, we extend the structural nested cumulative survival time model (SNCSTM), a causal survival model for time-varying treatments, to a cause-specific version. We show that the cause-specific SNCSTM is closely related to the Fine–Gray subdistribution hazard model, and we study identification under two frameworks. The first, based on sequential ignorability, applies when the time-varying treatment is distinct from the randomized assignment, as with oral PrEP use in AMP. The second, based on an instrumental variable, applies when the time-varying treatment represents compliance with the randomized assignment, and it accommodates unmeasured confounding between the time-varying treatment and event time. Under both frameworks, we propose consistent and asymptotically normal estimators of the structural parameters. We apply the methods to HVTN 704/HPTN 085, one of the two AMP trials, to estimate the effects of oral PrEP use and of VRC01 infusions administered every 8 weeks on acquisition of VRC01-sensitive and VRC01-insensitive HIV-1 strains. We also estimate the counterfactual strain-specific cumulative incidence curves under no oral PrEP use and under full adherence to the scheduled VRC01 infusions.
