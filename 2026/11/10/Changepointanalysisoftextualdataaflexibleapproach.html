Change-point analysis of textual data: a flexible approach


Bojana Milošević, Luka Perović
[stat.ME]

We propose a CUSUM-based change-point procedure for word-frequency vectors that accounts for the variation in the amount of observed textual information over time. Under a multinomial model, we derive the asymptotic null distribution of the proposed statistic, allowing for varying document numbers and lengths. With trimming based on cumulative word counts, the limiting null distribution does not depend on the temporal distribution of textual information. We also consider parametric and nonparametric bootstrap procedures for finite-sample calibration. A simulation study examines their performance under both multinomial sampling and Dirichlet mixtures of multinomial distributions. The results demonstrate the advantages of the proposed approach under increasing document intensity and show that resampling entire documents provides more reliable calibration in the presence of document-level heterogeneity. Finally, we apply the procedure, combined with binary segmentation, to 68,542 abstracts from the arXiv category stat.ML spanning 2007-2025, identifying multiple changes in word-usage patterns.
