Kernel Ridge Regression over Hölder Classes: Function and Derivative Losses


Yuxuan Hou, Yicheng Li
[stat.ML,stat.TH]

We study ordinary kernel ridge regression over full H"older-Zygmund balls, measuring errors in Sobolev norms and in pointwise and uniform losses for mixed derivatives. For targets of smoothness $s$, every RKHS norm equivalent to $H^{s+d/2}$ gives the optimal $H^r$ risk for $0\le r<s$; integer choices of $r$ include integrated squared derivative losses. Pointwise and uniform guarantees require additional kernel structure. A localized wavelet kernel attains the sharp derivative rates with the same regularization orders as for function estimation, whereas equivalent changes of the RKHS norm can destroy the population bias rates. For the ordinary Gaussian kernel with a shrinking bandwidth, we prove a uniform derivative risk bound with the optimal polynomial rate and an explicit, unoptimized logarithmic factor. The results allow bounded measurable design densities bounded away from zero and conditionally sub-Gaussian noise.
