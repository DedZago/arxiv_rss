ALDER: A Single-Loop Augmented-Lagrangian Damped Extragradient Method for Nonconvex--Concave Minimax Problems with Nonlinear Coupled Constraints


H. Zhang, M. Zhang, Z. Xu, Y. -H. Dai
[stat.ML]

ALDER, a single-loop augmented-Lagrangian damped extragradient method, is proposed for smooth nonconvex–concave minimax problems with nonlinear coupled inequality constraints. Within an augmented-Lagrangian framework, ALDER combines prediction–correction steps for the primal variables with explicit projected-and-relaxed multiplier updates in a single loop. The penalty parameter is fixed throughout each run, eliminating inner-loop subproblem solves and iterative penalty updates. We establish uniform boundedness of the relaxed multiplier sequence and, under an extended Mangasarian–Fromovitz constraint qualification, boundedness of the projected output multiplier. Building on these bounds, we show that ALDER finds an $\varepsilon$-KKT point within $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-7/2})$ complete first-order oracle evaluations in the strongly concave setting and $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-4})$ in the concave setting. These bounds improve upon the best known complexity bounds for multi-loop algorithms for this problem class, achieved by FAL, by removing a $\log(1/\varepsilon)$ factor, while retaining a single-loop update structure.
