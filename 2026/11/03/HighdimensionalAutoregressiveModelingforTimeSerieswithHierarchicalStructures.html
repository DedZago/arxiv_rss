High-dimensional Autoregressive Modeling for Time Series with Hierarchical Structures


Lan Li, Shibo Yu, Yingzhou Wang, Guodong Li
[stat.ME]

High-dimensional data, especially time-dependent data with increasingly complex structures, have become ubiquitous in modern applications, and hierarchical relationships among variables are increasingly common. However, there is still a lack of supervised learning tools in the literature for them. To fill this gap, we introduce a new model-designing framework, and it then combines with unsupervised factor modeling tools to form an efficient and interpretable autoregressive model for high-dimensional time series with hierarchical structures. An ordinary least squares estimation is considered, and its non-asymptotic properties are established. Moreover, we propose an algorithm to search for estimates, and a boosting method is also suggested for hyperparameter selection. Simulation experiments are conducted to evaluate finite-sample performance of the proposed methodology, and its usefulness is demonstrated by an application to the Personality-120 dataset.
