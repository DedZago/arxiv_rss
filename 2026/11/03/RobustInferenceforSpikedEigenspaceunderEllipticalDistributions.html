Robust Inference for Spiked Eigenspace under Elliptical Distributions


Zeyu Wang, Qing Yang, Xiao Han
[stat.ME]

In this paper, we study two eigenspace testing problems under high-dimensional elliptical spiked models: whether the spiked eigenspace equals a given subspace, and whether a given vector is a spike eigenvector. We propose a \textbf{Robust Functional Projection} (RFP) test and a \textbf{Robust Spike Direction} (RSD) test for these two problems, respectively. Based on the spatial-sign covariance matrix $\bm C_n$, both tests achieve robustness by remaining invariant to the radial distribution without moment conditions and allowing the background level to be unknown. To construct and analyze both tests, we establish central limit theorems for \textbf{G}eneralized \textbf{E}lliptical \textbf{S}patial-sign \textbf{S}pectral statistics (GESS) $\operatorname{tr}{f(\bm C_n)\bm B_n}$. The results cover a general class of deterministic weighting matrices $\bm B_n$ whose ranks are either comparable to the dimension or of smaller order, providing a common theoretical foundation for both tests. The central idea in constructing both tests is to avoid the need for consistent estimations of individual spike eigenvalues by working directly with GESS. Simulations illustrate the finite-sample performance of the procedures, and an analysis of LOCATA acoustic data demonstrates their use in assessing a signal subspace and a steering direction, both constructed from measured source and array geometry.
