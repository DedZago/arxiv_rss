Efficient Proper Agnostic Learning of Halfspaces


Anindya De, Elchanan Mossel, Shivam Nadimpalli, Joe Neeman
[stat.ML]

We give a proper membership-query algorithm for agnostically learning halfspaces under the uniform distribution on ${\pm1}^n$. Given query access to an arbitrary function $f:{\pm1}^n \to {\pm1}$, our algorithm runs in time $\mathrm{poly}(n)+2^{\mathrm{poly}(1/\varepsilon)}$ and outputs a halfspace with error at most $\mathrm{OPT}+\varepsilon$, where $\mathrm{OPT}$ is the error of the best-fitting halfspace for $f$. Previously, even with membership queries, the best known running time was $n^{\mathrm{poly}(1/\varepsilon)}$, achieved by the sample-based algorithm of Kalai, Klivans, Mansour, and Servedio (FOCS 2005). Our algorithm is the first agnostic halfspace learner under the uniform distribution on ${\pm1}^n$ whose running time is polynomial in $n$ with an exponent independent of $\varepsilon$. Combining our algorithm with a boosting argument yields a proper agnostic learner using only random examples. It achieves the same $\mathrm{OPT}+\varepsilon$ guarantee, with running time matching that of Kalai et al.\ up to an additive term depending only on $\varepsilon$. To our knowledge, this is the first sample-based proper agnostic learner in the Boolean setting with running time $2^{o(n)}$ for every fixed $\varepsilon>0$.
