Optimal Certified Multiple Testing with E-values


Soumyabrata Bose, Jay Bartroff
[stat.ME,stat.TH]

We study randomized multiple testing with e-values under arbitrary dependence and introduce a conditional certification constraint: Given the observed evidence, each coordinate’s expected rejection certificate must not exceed its e-value. Existing randomized e-BH improvements satisfy this constraint but have no established optimality guarantee. We prove that the maximum conditional inclusion probability for hypothesis $i$, given e-values~$x$, is $1 \wedge (\alpha / K) \sum_j \min(x_i, x_j)$, and construct one rule attaining these bounds simultaneously. The rule uses a single independent uniform variable, rejects a prefix of the ordered e-values, and has a unique conditional rejection-set law among rules attaining all the bounds. It therefore maximizes every nonnegative weighted sum of inclusion probabilities, including expected true discoveries under any truth configuration. Under a shared uniform draw, its rejection set contains those of e-BH and U-eBH. The construction extends to BY-calibrated p-values, and simulations under positive and negative dependence show power gains over existing randomized procedures.
