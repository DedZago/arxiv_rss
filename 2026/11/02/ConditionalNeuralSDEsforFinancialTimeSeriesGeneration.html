Conditional Neural SDEs for Financial Time-Series Generation


Sina Molavipour, Alireza M. Javid, Björn Löfdahl, Mikhail Nechaev
[stat.ML]

Conditional generation of time-series paths is a central problem in probabilistic modeling, where the goal is to simulate future trajectories that are both realistic and responsive to external context. It has direct applications in finance and economics. Neural stochastic differential equations provide a natural framework for this task by combining continuous-time stochastic dynamics with learnable drift and diffusion functions. However, directly modeling the target state can be unstable in conditional settings, especially when the target and conditioning variables have strong level dependence, limited training data, or different dimensions.We propose a residualanchor Neural SDE architecture that decomposes the target into a learned conditional anchor and a stochastic residual. The anchor captures the context-dependent baseline, while the Neural SDE (Stochastic Differential Equation) models the residual dynamics around this baseline. This separation reduces the burden on the stochastic dynamics and generalizes spread-based representations to more general conditioning settings.We evaluate the approach on real-world financial time series, including conditional interest-rate scenarios and a comparison with a time-series foundation model. The results show that the proposed model can generate plausible conditional paths, maintain competitive point accuracy, and provide calibrated uncertainty estimates.
