Anchored Stein Variational Gradient Descent Algorithm for Non-smooth Sampling


Thanh Dang, Matthew X. Burns, Jiaming Liang
[stat.ML]

We introduce a Stein variational framework for sampling from a target density $p\propto e^{-U}$ when the target potential $U$ is possibly non-smooth. The key idea is to introduce a smoother reference potential $U_0$ and the anchor weight $a=e^{U-U_0}$. The resulting anchored Stein variational gradient descent (SVGD) algorithm is able to target $p\propto e^{-U}$ without computing the gradient of $U$ and include standard SVGD as a special case. In particular, we allow the target potential $U$ to be locally Lipschitz while the reference potential $U_0$ is continuously differentiable, along with other suitable assumptions. Under this low regularity regime, we establish a Kullback-Leibler first variation formula for anchored perturbations, identify the corresponding steepest descent direction in the reproducing kernel Hilbert space and prove a nonasymptotic convergence bound for the anchored SVGD algorithm in terms of an anchored kernelized Stein discrepancy. Finally, we also provide guidance on constructing a suitable reference potential $U_0$ based on $U$.
