Latent Nonlinear Denoising Score Matching for Enhanced Learning of Structured Distributions


Kaichen Shen, Wei Zhu
[stat.ML]

Score-based generative models commonly employ linear forward diffusions with Gaussian reference distributions, limiting their ability to directly incorporate structural information into the noising dynamics. We introduce latent nonlinear denoising score matching (LNDSM), which combines nonlinear, structure-informed latent diffusions with the variational autoencoder (VAE) training framework. In our construction, the forward diffusion is designed to admit a prescribed reference distribution, such as a Gaussian mixture model, that reflects qualitative features of the latent data distribution. We derive a computable approximation to a cross-entropy upper bound in the VAE objective using Euler-Maruyama discretization and the resulting Gaussian conditional transitions. To stabilize training, we remove two zero-mean control variates whose variances become large for small time steps. The resulting objective enables joint training of the encoder, decoder, and latent score network. Numerical experiments on benchmark image datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of LNDSM in learning structured distributions, outperforming conventional latent score-based generative modeling in sample quality and mode balance across several settings, while achieving improved training efficiency. These results highlight the potential of nonlinear, structure-informed latent diffusions for generative modeling.
