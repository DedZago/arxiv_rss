Anytime Valid Confidence Bands for Distribution Functions


Bror Hultberg, Dave Zachariah, Antônio H. Ribeiro
[stat.ML]

Nonparametric inference often requires characterizing an unknown function, such as a cumulative distribution function (CDF), across its entire domain rather than a specific point. We develop confidence bands for distribution functions of a data stream that are valid simultaneously over all times and all points, even when each observation may depend arbitrarily on the past. We begin by deriving such bands for the time-averaged conditional CDF and show how their widths are determined by the depth of a binary search tree. We then show that this result is an instance of a framework through which one can construct valid confidence bands uniformly over time and over an arbitrary index set for any indexed family of time-averaged distribution functions. We illustrate the framework on importance-weighted and right-censored CDFs, as well as multivariate CDFs.
