Rao-Blackwellized e-variables


Dante de Roos, Ben Chugg, Peter Grünwald, Aaditya Ramdas
[stat.ME,stat.TH]

We show that for any concave utility, the expected utility of an e-variable can only increase after conditioning on a sufficient statistic, echoing the classic Rao-Blackwell theorem. The simplest form of the result has a straightforward proof using Jensen’s inequality, but we prove more sophisticated versions. We provide several applications of this insight, including a derivation of the log-optimal e-variable for testing the variance in a linear regression model.
