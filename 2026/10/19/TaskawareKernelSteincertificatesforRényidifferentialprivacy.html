Task-aware Kernel Stein certificates for Rényi differential privacy


Leoni Carla Wirth, Tara Trautwein, Gesine Reinert
[stat.ML]

R`enyi differential privacy, based on R`enyi divergence, has become a notion of choice for privacy guarantees. However, outside the Gaussian setting, R`enyi divergence can be difficult to compute. In particular, R`enyi differential privacy guarantees that exploit task-specific information, as in R`enyi Pufferfish privacy, can be challenging. Here we use kernel Stein discrepancies based on escort distributions to provide certificates for R`enyi differential privacy. By designing the kernel to focus on the relevant privacy directions, we also derive task-aware privacy certificates. We provide exact identities for Gaussian and Laplace mechanisms and bounds for exponential mechanisms, with separate task-aware guarantees. A real data set example illustrates how the protected comparison set and the released subspace jointly determine the resulting certificates.
