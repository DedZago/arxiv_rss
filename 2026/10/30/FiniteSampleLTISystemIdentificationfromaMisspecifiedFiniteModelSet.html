Finite-Sample LTI System Identification from a Misspecified Finite Model Set


Nicolas Chatzikiriakos, Andrea Iannelli
[stat.ML]

We consider the problem of finite-sample identification of an unknown LTI system from a finite hypothesis class. In contrast to existing results that assume the hypothesis class contains the true system, we consider the misspecified setting, where the true system does not belong to the hypothesis class. We first characterize the resulting estimation target and its dependence on the data-generation scheme. We then establish a finite-sample lower bound on the failure probability that any identification algorithm must incur. Finally, we analyze the robustness of an existing exponential weighting-based identification algorithm to the misspecification and derive a finite-sample upper bound on its failure probability. Our results illustrate when using a misspecified finite hypothesis class can provide an advantage over identification without prior knowledge of the candidate systems.
