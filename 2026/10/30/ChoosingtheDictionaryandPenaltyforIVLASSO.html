Choosing the Dictionary and Penalty for IV-LASSO


Yukun Ma, Manu Navjeevan, Bogdan Salahub
[stat.AP]

Estimating an instrumental variables (IV) first stage with the least absolute shrinkage and selection operator (LASSO) requires choosing a dictionary and penalty. Existing rules target first-stage objectives, although these choices affect structural estimation. We derive a feasible approximate mean squared error (AMSE) criterion for full-sample IV-LASSO with one endogenous regressor and homoskedastic Gaussian errors. For a prespecified list of dictionary-penalty pairs, the criterion trades off approximation of the optimal instrument against a complexity cost that depends on the first two moments of the LASSO effective dimension and on the degree of endogeneity. The selected pair attains the smallest candidate-specific AMSE component up to vanishing relative error. In simulations calibrated to Paravisini et al. (2015), the criterion improves substantially on the plug-in rule at low endogeneity and on cross-validation at high endogeneity, with nearly identical results under non-Gaussian errors. A supplementary appendix formally extends analysis to a class of non-Gaussian errors.
