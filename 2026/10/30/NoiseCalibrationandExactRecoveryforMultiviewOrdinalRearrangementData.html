Noise Calibration and Exact Recovery for Multiview Ordinal Rearrangement Data


Fanghua Chen, Yizhou Cai, Ting Fung Ma, Yun Yang
[stat.ME,stat.TH]

We studied simultaneous recovery of a latent group count, subject partition, and signature means from multiview ordinal ranks. A normalized reference rearrangement score is an affine Spearman correlation; the problem concerns the information retained by several such scores and their noise geometry. We develop an ordered-gap estimator with direction-specific concentration radii. Its finite-sample guarantee separates signature noise, missing groups, and candidate coverage, and allows the group count to grow under an attainable-margin condition. A coordinatewise partition followed by label-tuple refinement recovers groups even when every individual coordinate has population collisions. A binary-code construction explains why the one-dimensional inverse-group margin restriction need not govern a retained coordinate bank. We derive actual-law concentration from bounded ranking modules and a fixed-dimensional combinatorial central limit theorem under an observed permutation representation. A binary ordinal submodel gives logarithmic exact-recovery information scales. A subject-effect construction distinguishes persistent overlap from reducible measurement noise, and an additional ordinal anchor quantifies how new information lowers that error floor. Five focused simulation scenarios illustrate direction-specific calibration, growing-group recovery, coordinate collision resolution, variance reduction, and the effect of an additional ordinal anchor.
