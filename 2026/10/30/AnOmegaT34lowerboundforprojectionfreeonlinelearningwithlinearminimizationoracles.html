An $\Omega(T^{3/4})$ lower bound for projection-free online learning with linear minimization oracles


Julien Weibel, Pierre Gaillard, Wouter Koolen, Adrien Taylor
[stat.ML]

This work considers projection-free online learning algorithms with linear minimization oracles for handling the decision set. For Online Frank–Wolfe (OFW), regret upper bounds were already known for specific OFW tunings [Hazan and Kale, 2012, Hazan, 2016] which scaled as $T^{3/4}$ (where $T$ is the horizon length). However, it remained an open question whether the regret of all OFW tunings scaled as $T^{3/4}$. We answer that question, proving a universal worst-case regret lower bound $\Omega(T^{3/4} m^{-1/4})$ for any projection-free algorithm that makes at most $m$ linear minimization oracle calls per round. The analysis suggests an extended OFW to resort to multiple linear optimizations per round, for which we prove a regret upper bound $O(\max { T^{3/4} m^{-1/4}, \sqrt{T} })$.
