Estimating Great Expectations Under Autoregressive Language Models with Potentials


Francesco I. Re, Shubhangi Ghosh, Tim Vieira, Ryan Cotterell
[stat.ME]

Many applications of language models require estimating the expectation of a test functional under the model. Obtaining precise estimates can be computationally expensive. We show how to improve estimation precision by exploiting next-token conditional probabilities available as a by-product of sampling. Our approach uses potentials: real-valued functions of prefixes that induce an additive decomposition of the test functional. We construct a family of estimators parameterized by these potentials and derive conditions for variance reduction. Across applications including entropy estimation, watermark detection, and rare-event probability estimation, our estimators achieve substantial variance reductions relative to Monte Carlo and importance sampling baselines at comparable computational cost.
