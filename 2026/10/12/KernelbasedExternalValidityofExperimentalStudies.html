Kernel-based External Validity of Experimental Studies


Federico Matteucci, Gerhard Neumann
[stat.ME,stat.ML]

The external validity of a study is the extent to which its findings generalize to unseen experimental conditions, such as datasets. We formalize the results of a study together with its research question, and define external validity as the probability that two independent and same-sized replications of the study produce results whose maximum mean discrepancy (MMD) is below a given tolerance. This approach, centered on the choice of a space of results and of an appropriate reproducing kernel for the MMD, is flexible enough to be deployed on multiple different types of results and research questions. Additionally, we establish an asymptotic power-law relation between the size of a study and the quantiles of the MMD, which we use to estimate the desired size of an externally valid study from a small-scale preliminary study. We deploy our framework to rankings with ties and discuss kernels suited to different research questions. Experiments on existing case studies and on a simulated newly designed study illustrate the applicability of the framework. We release our code as part of the genexpy Python module.
