Gaussian BROJA Partial Information Decomposition in Closed Form


Ohad Sheelo, Tal Golan, Ari Pakman
[stat.ML]

The BROJA partial information decomposition provides an operationally motivated way to separate the information that two sources carry about a target into unique, redundant, and synergistic components. For continuous and high-dimensional variables, however, computing the BROJA decomposition requires solving a difficult optimization problem. Previous work showed that, when each source-target pair is jointly Gaussian, the problem can be reduced to finite-dimensional numerical optimization. Here, we derive a closed-form solution to this optimization problem, enabling direct computation of the BROJA decomposition in the general multivariate Gaussian setting. The resulting closed-form expression enables speedups exceeding two orders of magnitude for high-dimensional Gaussian BROJA PID. Code is available at https://github.com/brainsandmachines/Eigen_PID.
