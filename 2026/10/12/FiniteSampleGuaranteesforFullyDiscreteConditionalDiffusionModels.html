Finite-Sample Guarantees for Fully Discrete Conditional Diffusion Models


Qiuyun Zhu
[stat.ML]

We establish finite-sample guarantees for conditional diffusion models learned from data and implemented with finitely many denoising steps. Our approach aligns the score-training objective with the sampling grid, allowing statistical estimation and numerical discretization errors to be analyzed together. Under a smooth conditional density model, we derive high-probability bounds on the generated distribution’s error and quantify the required number of score evaluations. For every smoothness index $\beta>0$, a suitable grid yields averaged conditional Kullback–Leibler (KL) divergence $\widetilde O_p(n^{-2\beta/(d_x+d_y+2\beta)})$ relative to the original target with or without early stopping. Both the full and early-stopped samplers attain average target total-variation (TV) error $\widetilde O_p(n^{-\beta/(d_x+d_y+2\beta)})$. Here $n$ is the sample size, $d_x,d_y$ are the data and conditioning dimensions, and errors are averaged over the conditioning variable. The total-variation bound matches the rate established in existing conditional distribution-estimation theory, up to logarithmic factors.
