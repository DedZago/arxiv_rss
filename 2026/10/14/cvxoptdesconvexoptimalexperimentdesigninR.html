cvxoptdes: convex optimal experiment design in R


Joris Chau, Alexandre Segers, Martin Otava, Stephen Karanja
[stat.ME,stat.CO]

The cvxoptdes R-package provides a unified framework to perform convex optimal experiment design and online response optimization for linear, generalized linear and additive, nonlinear, and random intercept mixed models, using exclusively open-source solvers. It supports optimization and augmentation of weighted, penalized and convex constrained continuous designs across a discrete grid of candidate points and rounding to integer-valued designs using point-exchange algorithms or mixed-integer programming for orthogonally constrained designs, including the class of orthogonal minimally aliased response surface (OMARS) designs. Models and design constraints are defined and updated in an object-oriented context with the aim to facilitate the process of sequential (penalized) design augmentation analogous to online design procedures known from active learning and Bayesian optimization.
