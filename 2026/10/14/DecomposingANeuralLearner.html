Decomposing A Neural Learner


Zelong Bi
[stat.ML]

Neural learners for classification and regression are often criticized as black boxes. We propose a probabilistic decomposition of neural learners that accounts for heterogeneous predictability across the feature space. A training procedure combining the EM algorithm, stochastic gradient descent, and maximum likelihood estimation is developed to jointly learn the predictable component and an input-dependent reliability score. We show that the optimal decomposed learner recovers the response distribution within the predictable component, while the reliability score characterizes the predictability of individual observations. We further extend the framework to incorporate localized feature transformations. Experiments on synthetic and real-world datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework.
