Extrinsic MANOVA for Oriented Projective Shapes


Musab Alamoudi, Robert L. Paige, Victor Patrangenaru
[stat.ME,stat.OT]

For configurations of landmarks reconstructed from camera images, classical projective shape analysis identifies antipodal spherical representatives of projective coordinates, while oriented projective shape (OPS) retains their distinction. In 3D, a fixed oriented-projective coordinate representation of a OPS is point on product of 3D spheres. We develop nonparametric multisample inference for equality of population extrinsic means on this product. Quaternion multiplication helps create a global orthonormal tangent frame field, in which we obtain explicit extrinsic covariance blocks that preserve dependence between registered landmarks. A covariance-weighted MANOVA statistic has a chi-square null limit under nonfocality of distributions and positive-definite extrinsic covariance assumptions. An inverse-free discrepancy has a weighted chi-square limit when covariance matrices may be singular, and centered, groupwise nonparametric bootstrap procedures are justified asymptotically. We apply a fixed-observed-covariance nonparametric bootstrap version of the weighted statistic to 31 seven-landmark facial reconstructions from five subjects. Separate tests at the right inner brow and chin reject equality of the five OPS mean directions after Bonferroni adjustment. The analysis concerns a common mixed-sign frame domain and marginal landmarks; it does not establish pairwise subject differences or a joint six-dimensional facial test.
