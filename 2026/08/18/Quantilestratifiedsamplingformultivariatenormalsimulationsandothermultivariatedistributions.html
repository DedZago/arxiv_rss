Quantile-stratified sampling for multivariate normal simulations and other multivariate distributions


Ben O'Neill
[stat.ME,stat.CO,stat.OT,stat.TH]

In this paper we show how to extend quantile-stratified sampling to produce simulations from various multivariate distributions. These simulations have desirable space-filling and coverage properties relative to simulation using IID sampling. We examine the coverage performance of these simulations against IID sampling by looking at plots of ordered log-density values from the simulations.
