Learnability of high-dimensional targets by two-parameter models and gradient flow


Dmitry Yarotsky
[stat.ML]

We explore the theoretical possibility of learning $d$-dimensional targets with $W$-parameter models by gradient flow (GF) when $W<d$ there is necessarily a large subset of GF-non-learnable targets. In particular, the set of learnable targets is not dense in $\mathbb R^d$, and any subset of $\mathbb R^d$ homeomorphic to the $W$-dimensional sphere contains non-learnable targets. Finally, we observe that the model in our main theorem on almost guaranteed two-parameter learning is constructed using a hierarchical procedure and as a result is not expressible by a single elementary function. We show that this limitation is essential in the sense that most models written in terms of elementary functions cannot achieve the learnability demonstrated in this theorem.
